I'm Mehreen, an AI/ML Engineer currently interning with the AIRI Team at PITB, based at Arfa Software Technology Park. My work centers on taking machine learning out of the notebook and into products — systems with real interfaces, real data pipelines, and real users in mind.

My most recent build, Punjab GeoGuardian, reflects that approach: a satellite-based land-monitoring platform that pairs Google Earth Engine's geospatial analysis with a FastAPI backend and a live interactive dashboard — not just a chart in a notebook, but a tool someone could actually open and use.

I care about the full stack of an AI idea: the model, the data it's fed, the API that serves it, and the interface that makes it usable by someone who isn't a data scientist.
