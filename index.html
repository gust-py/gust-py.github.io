GAME IN DEVELOPMENT — A LOST DRIVE

I'm gust-py, a Roblox Builder & Scripter focused on realism. My specialty is vehicles: realistic car chassis and functional suspensions that actually react to terrain, not just static decorations bolted onto a body.

I've also shipped a realistic grab system with fully functional arms — physics-driven, character-aware, and built to feel natural instead of floaty. On the building side, I care about detail: surfaces that tell a story, maps with atmosphere, environments that make players feel like they're somewhere real.

Right now I'm developing A Lost Drive, a desert trip game — an atmospheric drive through the dunes where the vehicle, the environment and the mood are the whole experience. And I'm always open to learning more: every system I build teaches me something new.
