GAME IN DEVELOPMENT — A LOST DRIVE

I'm gust-py, a Roblox Builder & Scripter obsessed with realism. My specialty is vehicles: realistic car chassis and functional suspensions that actually react to terrain — not static decorations bolted onto a body.

I've also developed a super-realistic procedural terrain system — endless, naturally generated landscapes with realistic dunes, slopes and surface detail. Combined with a realistic grab system with fully functional arms, my systems are built to feel physical and alive.

Right now I'm developing A Lost Drive, a desert trip game — an atmospheric drive through procedurally generated dunes where the vehicle, the terrain and the mood are the whole experience. And I'm always open to learning more: every system teaches me something new.
